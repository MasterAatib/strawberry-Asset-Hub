🤖 AI + Assets: Speed Up 2D Animation Development

Combining pre‑made assets with AI tools can drastically reduce production time. Here's a practical workflow:

1

Concept & Ideation

Use AI image generators (e.g., Midjourney, DALL‑E) to quickly explore visual styles and character designs. Generate a mood board before you even open your animation software.

2

Select Base Assets

Browse Strawberry Asset Hub for character sprites, backgrounds, or effect animations that match your concept. Choose assets that are close to your vision — even if they need slight modifications.

3

AI‑Assisted Refinement

Use tools like Runway ML or Pika to generate in‑between frames (tweening) or to upscale/reskin existing sprites. For character animations, you can use AI motion capture plugins to generate new poses from your base sprites.

4

Integrate & Iterate

Import the refined assets into your game engine (Unity, Unreal, Godot). Use the engine's animation tools to blend AI‑generated frames with your static sprites. Test, tweak, and repeat — AI gives you rapid prototypes, but you still have full creative control.

Pro tip: Combine AI with your assets to create variations. For example, take a base weapon sprite and use AI style transfer to generate multiple colour palettes or theme variants — all from a single asset.
